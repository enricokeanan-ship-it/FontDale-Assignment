FontDale began as a bold vision by our founder, Franz Diego, in 2023. The goal was simple yet ambitious: to create a premium lifestyle destination that blends modern design with high-quality experiences. Strategically located at Arcadia Circle, FontDale was designed to be more than just a shopping center; it was built to be a standout landmark for the modern urban shopper.

THE VISIONARY

In 2025, FontDale officially opened its doors to the world. The mall is uniquely structured into two main wings: the North Gallery and the South Pavilion. These two areas are seamlessly connected by our signature Lumen Skywalk, an architectural highlight that symbolizes our commitment to contemporary and innovative design.

Franz Diego

Founder & Chairman, FontDale Lifestyle Mall

"FontDale is not just a mall — it is a statement of how modern life should feel."

MODERN LANDMARK

Today, FontDale serves as a vibrant platform where visitors can explore global fashion brands, discover diverse dining experiences, and participate in exciting events. Whether you are here for the latest trends or a premium lifestyle atmosphere, FontDale continues to strengthen its presence as a destination of elegance and convenience.
